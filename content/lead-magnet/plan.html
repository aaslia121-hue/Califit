A no-nonsense beginner plan. All you need is a bar and a resistance band. Three sessions a week, about 25 minutes each.

How to use this: do the test on page 2, follow the week that matches your level, and re-test at the end of week 4. Swinging doesn't count. Every rep is strict.

Where to start

  • Hang under 20 s: start at Week 1 and repeat it once if it feels hard.
  • Hang over 30 s and negative over 3 s: start at Week 2.
  • Already 1–2 strict reps: start at Week 3 and add 1 strict rep per set each week.

Strict pull-up checklist

  • Start every rep from a full dead hang.
  • Pull your shoulders down first, then drive your elbows to your ribs.
  • Keep your legs together, squeeze your glutes, and don't swing or kick.
  • Get your chin over the bar, then lower for 3 seconds.

Recovery rules

  • Eat roughly 1.6 g of protein per kg of bodyweight a day.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours. That's when the strength actually gets built.
  • Sharp joint pain means stop. Muscle soreness is normal.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping negatives because they're hard. They're the fastest way to build the pull.
  • Training to failure every session. Leave 1–2 reps in the tank.
  • Rushing to remove the band. Earn it, one lighter band at a time.
