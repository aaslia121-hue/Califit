# @califitcoach_amit: 4-Week Content Plan
**16 Nov – 13 Dec 2026** · 5 feed posts a week (Mon–Fri) · Stories every day

## Strategy
- **Goal:** grow followers, then capture leads with **START** (free pull-up plan PDF), then convert with **COACH** (1:1 DM).
- **Theme of the month:** *"Your first pull-up."* One clear topic makes you known for something.
- **Mix each week:** 2 education, 1 opinion or entertainment, 1 lifestyle, 1 story or proof.
- **Post time:** 6–8 pm local. Check your Insights and move it to when your followers are most active.
- **Batch day:** film everything for the coming week on **Sunday** (about 2 hours).

**CTA rotation:**
| CTA | Use for |
|---|---|
| "Comment **START** for my free pull-up plan" | Education posts (lead magnet) |
| "**DM me COACH**" | Story and proof posts (sales) |
| "Save this for your next session" | Tutorials (reach) |
| "Send this to your gym buddy" | Skits and opinions (shares) |

## Daily Stories (keep it light, 3–5 frames)
- **Mon:** poll, for example "Can you do a strict pull-up? Yes / Not yet"
- **Tue:** reshare the day's post + link sticker to the free plan
- **Wed:** behind the scenes of a training session
- **Thu:** Q&A box, "Ask me anything about pull-ups" (answers become future reels)
- **Fri:** win of the week: a client or follower repost
- **Sat/Sun:** casual life, meal prep, Sunday filming day

## Weekly routine
1. **Sun:** film the week's 5 posts and write captions.
2. **Daily:** post at your set time, then spend 15 min replying to every comment (send START DMs).
3. **Fri:** check Insights. Note the top post by shares and saves, and make another version of it next month.
