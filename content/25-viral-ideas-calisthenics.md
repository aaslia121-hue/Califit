# Califit: 25 Viral Content Ideas, Built for Calisthenics

Each of the 25 formats from the reference carousel, adapted for Califit. For each one: the **hook** (on-screen text or first line spoken), a **shot plan**, and a **caption and call to action**. Film vertical (9:16) unless noted.

---

## Transformation and story

### 1. Before/after with trending audio
- **Hook:** "Day 1: 0 pull-ups. Day 365: 👇"
- **Shots:** an old clip of a failed pull-up or dead hang, then a hard cut on the beat drop to a muscle-up or 15 strict pull-ups. Same angle and the same spot if you can.
- **Caption:** "No gym. Just a bar and consistency. Comment START and I'll send you the beginner plan."

### 12. Voiceover transformation story
- **Hook (voice):** "A year ago I couldn't hold a 10-second plank."
- **Shots:** B-roll of old photos and clips, then current training. Calm voiceover with three beats: the low point, the turning point, the habit that stuck.
- **Call to action:** "Save this for the day you want to quit."

### 19. Year-by-year transformation carousel
- **Slides:** cover ("My calisthenics journey, year by year"), one slide per year with a photo, one skill unlocked and one lesson, then a closing slide with your current goal.
- **Caption:** "Which year are you in right now?"

### 25. How I got into fitness
- **Hook:** "I started calisthenics because I couldn't afford a gym."
- **Shots:** talking head intercut with park and bar footage. End on what it changed: confidence, discipline, community.

### 24. Client result with a dramatic hook
- **Hook:** "She was told she'd never do a pull-up."
- **Shots:** the client's first attempt, then a progression montage, then a clean rep. Break down three things that worked, such as negatives, band work and frequency.
- **Call to action:** "Want the same plan? Link in bio."

## Education

### 2. Four ways to train one muscle
- **Hook:** "4 ways to build your back with zero equipment"
- **Shots:** four clean clips, such as pull-ups, Australian rows, a towel row on a door and a superman hold. Put a label on screen for each.

### 8. Step-by-step exercise tutorial (multiple shots)
- **Hook:** "How to get your first muscle-up in 4 steps"
- **Shots:** for each step, film a wide shot, a side angle and a close-up of the grip or wrist. Steps: explosive pull-up, then high pull to the chest, then the transition on a low bar, then the full rep.

### 11. Step-by-step presentation on laptop or iPad
- **Hook:** "The exact plan to lose 5 kg with bodyweight training"
- **Shots:** screen-record slides (deficit, protein, 3× full-body per week, daily steps) with a picture-in-picture of your face.

### 15. Triple-clone exercise variations
- **Hook:** "Easy, medium, savage push-ups"
- **Shots:** a locked-off tripod shot filmed three times and composited: incline push-up, standard push-up, archer push-up. Also works for squats (air squat, Bulgarian split squat, pistol squat).

### 5. "Stop doing this" plus an alternative
- **Hook:** "Stop kipping your pull-ups if you want strength."
- **Shots:** the bad rep with a red ✕, the fix with a green ✓, and a 3-point cue list.

### 10. Supplement breakdown
- **Hook:** "The only 3 supplements a calisthenics athlete needs"
- **Shots:** flat-lay on a gym bench with creatine, whey and vitamin D or omega-3. One line each on why. Keep claims evidence-based.

### 9. Same calories, different volume
- **Hook:** "500 calories vs 500 calories"
- **Shots:** overhead split. One side is a pastry; the other is a bowl of chicken, rice, vegetables and fruit.

## Opinions and engagement

### 3. React to a viral exercise
- **Shots:** green-screen the viral clip, then give your verdict: "Useful or useless?"

### 4. Hottest take
- **Hook:** "Hot take: you don't need weights to build muscle."
- **Shots:** a fast talking head with three supporting points. Invite debate in the comments.

### 6. Rate topics 1–10 (start with the most controversial)
- **Order:** "Cardio for fat loss: 3/10" first, then L-sit, human flag, and so on.

### 7. Tier list
- **Shots:** "Calisthenics skills tier list, S to F." Drag the skills into tiers on screen.

### 13. Beginner vs pro skit
- **Shots:** you play both roles, with a wardrobe change (hoodie vs tank). Beginner: "I'll do 100 push-ups a day." Pro: "Do 3 hard sets and progress them."

### 14. Clone video: noob vs pro
- **Shots:** the same move side by side, such as a noob's dead hang next to a pro's front lever, or a sagging plank next to a hollow body hold.

### 23. Split-screen AI carousel: bad vs good habits
- **Slides:** pairs of contrasts such as "Skips warm-up vs mobilises wrists" and "Ego reps vs full range of motion." Use AI-generated or styled images with a consistent look.

## Lifestyle and documenting

### 16. Day in the life
- **Shots:** a timestamped montage of wake-up, breakfast, park session, work, meal prep, stretching and bed.

### 17. Morning or night routine
- **Hook:** "My 6am routine that built a 20-second handstand"
- **Shots:** water, 10 minutes of mobility, handstand practice, then breakfast.

### 18. What I eat in a day (shred or bulk)
- **Shots:** show each meal with its macros on screen and the daily total at the end.

### 20. React to a viral recipe and try it
- **Shots:** viral clip, then you cooking it, then a taste test, then a "calisthenics-approved?" verdict with a protein score.

### 21. React to a viral exercise and try it
- **Shots:** viral clip, then your attempt, unedited even if you fail. Honest attempts tend to perform well.

### 22. Landscape motivational, posted vertical
- **Shots:** cinematic wide shots at golden hour in a park, posted letterboxed in 9:16 with one line of text, for example "Nobody's coming to save you. Grab the bar."
